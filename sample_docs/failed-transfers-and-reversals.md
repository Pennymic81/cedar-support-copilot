# Failed transfers and reversals

Cedar Pay is a fictional company used to demonstrate the support copilot. These policies are sample content.

## Why a transfer can fail

A transfer can fail if the recipient's account details are wrong, the receiving bank rejects the payment, or the recipient's account is closed or restricted.

## What happens to your money

If you were charged for a transfer that failed, the full amount, including any fee, is returned to your Cedar Pay wallet automatically. Reversals usually arrive within 24 hours.

## If the money hasn't come back

If a reversal hasn't arrived after 24 hours, contact support with the transaction reference shown on your receipt. A member of the team will investigate the transfer with the receiving bank. Support will never ask for your password or full card number.

## Cancelling a transfer

You can cancel a transfer in the app while its status is "Processing". Once a transfer shows "Sent", it can't be cancelled.
