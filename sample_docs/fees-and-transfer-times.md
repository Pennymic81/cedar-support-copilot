# Fees and transfer times

Cedar Pay is a fictional company used to demonstrate the support copilot. These policies are sample content.

## Fees

Opening a Cedar Pay account is free and there is no monthly fee.

International transfers cost a flat fee of $1.99, shown before you confirm. The exchange rate is also shown before you send, so you always know exactly how much the recipient gets.

Paying bills and buying airtime from your wallet is free.

## Transfer times

Most transfers to banks in Europe, including Spain, the UK and Germany, arrive within minutes. Some banks can take up to 1 business day.

Transfers to the United States usually arrive within 1 business day.

Transfers sent on weekends or public holidays may arrive on the next business day.

## Holding more than one currency

You can hold balances in US dollars, euros and British pounds, and convert between them in the app at the rate shown.
