# Account tiers and limits

Cedar Pay is a fictional company used to demonstrate the support copilot. These policies are sample content.

## Tier 1 accounts

Every new Cedar Pay account starts on Tier 1. You can open a Tier 1 account with just your phone number and email address.

On Tier 1 you can send up to $500 per day and hold a balance of up to $2,000 across all currencies.

## Tier 2 accounts

To upgrade to Tier 2, verify your identity in the app by uploading a government-issued photo ID and taking a selfie. Most verifications are approved within 10 minutes; some take up to 1 business day.

On Tier 2 you can send up to $2,000 per day and hold a balance of up to $20,000.

## Raising your limit further

Business accounts and customers who need higher limits can contact support to request a review. Higher limits are not guaranteed and depend on account history.

## When a limit is reached

If a transfer would take you over your daily limit, the app shows how much you can still send today. Daily limits reset at midnight UTC.
